Material Management & Costing System: Work Instruction

How material is set up, received, issued, scrapped, counted, charged to heats, reconciled and costed at Vijay Spheroidals Pvt. Ltd. Every rule on this page is taken from the application code. All figures are DEMO values for training, not plant data.

One posting path Stock changes only when an approved Stores document (Opening Balance, GRN, Issue, Waste or Physical Audit) posts to the append-only stock ledger. The Foundry heat log is a production record. It is compared against issues but never deducts stock.

1. End-to-end flow

Nine stages in order. The approval stage is the only gate into the ledger; the heat log stage writes no stock movement.

  1. MastersDepartments, UOMs, categories, vendors, item codes, aliases
  2. Opening balanceCertified count at 12 Oct 2026, posted once by Admin
  3. GRNStores records receipt with rate ex GST
  4. ApprovalManagement approves; creator cannot approve
  5. Stock ledgerAppend-only movement, negative stock refused
  6. Issue slipThe only stock deduction for furnace material
  7. Heat logFoundry charge, tapped weight, parts. No posting
  8. ReconciliationIssued vs charged per heat and item
  9. Costing runStarted manually; weighted average, heat and part cost

Waste / Scrap and Physical Audit follow the same path as GRN and Issue: create, submit, approve, post.

3. Step-by-step procedure

Each step names who does it, who approves it, and what it does to stock.

  1. Request a missing item code

    Stores, Foundry or Purchase raise · Management approves or rejects

    • Fill name, department, matching category, UOM and justification. Status DRAFT, then SUBMITTED.
    • The system blocks a name that already exists as an item, an alias or an open request.
    • On approval the code is generated and a registry row records serial, standard version and approver.

    item_code_service.py, item_nomenclature.py

  2. Map shop-floor names to items (aliases)

    Stores or Foundry create · Management approves

    • Heat log and Excel lines are matched to items through approved aliases. An unmatched heat charge line is marked UNMAPPED_ALIAS and left out of costing.

    material_alias_service.py

  3. Set opening stock

    Rate proposed and approved by two different people · quantity posted by Admin

    • Quantity comes only from a management-certified physical count at the cutover.
    • Preview first. Posting is refused if any row has a blocking error or the baseline date is not 12 Oct 2026.
    • Creates OPENING_BALANCE ledger movements.

    opening_balance_service.py, opening_valuation_service.py

  4. Receive material (GRN)

    Stores creates and submits · Management approves

    • Header: receipt date, source type (External vendor, Internal VSPL or Segregation yard), vendor, invoice number and date. Number format GRN-YYYYMMDD-XXXX.
    • Lines: item, UOM, quantity, rate ex GST, GST %. Rate is optional in the form but a line without a rate posts stock unvalued.
    • The system checks for duplicate invoices and duplicate lines.
    • DRAFT, then SUBMITTED, then APPROVED. The creator cannot approve.
    • On approval: + INWARD_RECEIPT (or INTERNAL_INWARD) in the ledger. GST is stored but costing uses the rate ex GST.

    grn_service.py, duplicate_detector.py, ledger_service.post_grn_to_ledger

  5. Issue material

    Stores creates and submits · Management approves

    • Header: issue date, issuing and receiving department, heat reference number for furnace material. Number format ISS-YYYYMMDD-XXXX.
    • On approval: the item rows are locked, the balance is checked, and − OUTWARD_ISSUE is posted.
    • If the issue would make stock negative, approval is refused with "Insufficient inventory". Nothing is posted.

    issue_service.py, ledger_service.post_issue_to_ledger

  6. Record waste or scrap

    Stores logs · Management approves

    • Waste type: Borings, Gatings/Runners, Returns, Rejected castings, Slag, Other scrap. Number format WST-YYYYMMDD-XXXX.
    • Disposition decides the ledger effect: Scrap outward posts − quantity; Internal recovery posts a zero-quantity record; Inspection hold posts nothing.

    waste_service.py, ledger_service.post_waste_to_ledger

  7. Count stock (physical audit)

    Stores creates and submits · Management approves

    • Variance = physical count − system quantity at cutoff. On approval the variance posts as PHYSICAL_AUDIT_ADJUSTMENT. Approval is blocked if the live balance moved after the cutoff snapshot.

    physical_audit_service.py

  8. Log the heat

    Foundry (or Management) records · no approval step

    • Header: heat number (original kept, normalised key used for matching), heat date, furnace, shift, metal tapped weight.
    • Charge lines: description as written, quantity, UOM. Repeat additions of the same item are separate lines and are added together later.
    • Parts produced: description, quantity, unit cast weight, Good / Rejected / Rework.
    • No ledger movement is written.

    heat_service.py, heat_import_service.py

  9. Reconcile issues against heats

    Foundry or Management run · Management resolves exceptions

    • Pass 1 exact heat and slip, Pass 2 same-day batch, Pass 3 fuzzy suggestions only (never auto-confirmed).
    • Each rerun is a new snapshot; old results are kept.

    reconciliation_service.py

  10. Run costing

    Management or Admin start the run manually

    • Rebuilds each item's weighted-average rate from the ledger, then values heats and parts. Material cost only.
    • Statuses explain anything not valued (missing rate, missing opening valuation, missing denominator).

    costing_service.execute_costing_run

  11. Correct a mistake

    Management or Admin

    • Posted entries are never edited. Reverse the GRN, Issue or Waste; the system writes linked REVERSAL entries.

    ledger_service.reverse_*

5. Formula register

Solid boxes are calculated by the backend. Dashed boxes are screen estimates only and do not feed costing.

Stock on hand

Closing Qty = Σ quantity_delta of all ledger entries for the item

Opening balance, receipts, issues, scrap, audit adjustments and reversals are all signed entries in one ledger.

Moving weighted-average rate (on each GRN line with rate > 0)

If stock before receipt ≤ 0:   New Rate = Receipt Rate
Else:   New Rate = round4( (Qty × Rate + Receipt Qty × Receipt Rate) ÷ (Qty + Receipt Qty) )

Starts from the approved opening rate. A GRN line without a rate adds quantity but does not change the rate (status MISSING_RECEIPT_RATE). Internal inward adds quantity with valuation policy pending. Same-day order: opening, receipts, positive adjustments, issues and scrap, negative adjustments, reversals.

Issue and scrap value

Issue Value = round2( Issued Qty × Current Average Rate )

Issues do not change the average rate.

Reconciliation tolerance and class

Issued  = Σ approved issue qty for (heat, item)
Charged = Σ heat charge qty for (heat, item)
Variance = Issued − Charged
Tolerance = 0.5 if Issued ≤ 0, else max(0.5, 0.02 × Issued)

1. Issued > 0 and Charged > 0 and Issued ÷ Charged ≥ 9.5 or ≤ 0.105  → DECIMAL_SUSPECT
2. |Variance| ≤ Tolerance                                                 → OK
3. Variance > Tolerance                                                  → EXCESS_ISSUE
4. otherwise                                                              → SHORT_ISSUE

The 2% and 0.5 are fixed in code. Unmatched or ambiguous rows get UNMATCHED_SLIP, UNMATCHED_HEAT or AMBIGUOUS_MATCH.

Heat material cost per kg

Line Cost = round2( Charged Qty × Item Average Rate as of heat date )
Heat Material Cost = Σ Line Cost (valued lines only)
Denominator = Metal Tapped Weight            (default, METAL_TAPPED)
           or Σ Good Qty × Unit Cast Weight  (GOOD_CASTING_KG)
Cost per kg = round4( Heat Material Cost ÷ Denominator )

Status COMPLETE only if every charge line has a mapped item and a rate; otherwise PARTIAL_COST or INSUFFICIENT_COST_DATA. No denominator gives DENOMINATOR_UNAVAILABLE. Power and consumables are not included.

Part material cost

Cost per Part = round4( Unit Cast Weight × Heat Cost per kg )
Total = round2( Quantity × Cost per Part )

Calculated only when the heat status is COMPLETE and the part has a unit cast weight.

Opening value

Opening Value = Certified Opening Qty × Approved Opening Rate ex GST

Melt loss and yield (screen estimate only)

Melt Loss kg = Σ charge qty − Metal Tapped Weight
Melt Loss %  = Melt Loss kg ÷ Σ charge qty × 100
Warning above 6.5 %, critical above 8.0 %

Shown on the Heat Log screen only. Not stored or used by the costing run. The same screen's cost per kg uses fixed estimates and will not match a costing run.
