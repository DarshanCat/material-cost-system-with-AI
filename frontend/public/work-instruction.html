Material Management & Costing System (MMS) · Standard Operating Work Instruction & Approval Workflow
WI-MM-001 · Rev 4.0 (Live Published)

🛡️ Fundamental Principle: Segregation of Duties (Maker-Checker Enforced)

In compliance with Indian Accounting Standard (AS 2) and Companies (Accounts) Rules, creators of documents can NEVER approve their own transactions. Operational entries (GRN, Issue Slips, Waste Dispositions, Physical Audits) are drafted by department makers and require explicit verification by an independent Authoritative Checker (Management) before posting to the immutable stock ledger.

Workflow 1: Purchase Order to Goods Receipt Note (GRN) Procurement Flow

Strict 3-Way Match enforcement: Goods can only be received against approved purchase rates, preventing rate drift and unrecorded liabilities.

1 Raise Purchase Order

PO Drafting (Purchase Dept)

Purchase team selects approved vendor from 12-Vendor Master, inputs required item codes, quantities, and negotiated rate.

👤 usr_purchase_01 Status: DRAFT
2 Commercial Approval

Rate Lock & PO Approval

Management reviews rate, payment terms, and delivery schedules. On approval, the PO is locked and transmitted to vendor.

👑 usr_mgmt_01 Status: APPROVED
3 Gate Inward & Weighment

GRN Creation (Stores Dept)

Stores inspects vehicle, weighs consignment on weighbridge, verifies vendor Delivery Challan (DC), and links the GRN to the approved PO.

👤 usr_stores_01 Status: SUBMITTED (Pending QC)
4 Quality Inspection

Lab Testing & Heat Spec Verification

Quality checks chemical spectrometer test certificates (C, Si, Mn, S, P) or physical dimensions before accepting heat charge.

🔬 Quality Lead QC Passed / Accepted
5 Checker Approval

Management Approval & Stock Ledger Posting

Management verifies GRN vs PO rate. On approval, the backend automatically creates an immutable stock ledger INWARD entry.

👑 usr_mgmt_01 ⚡ Ledger Posted (Qty + Rate)
Workflow 2: Stores Material Issue to Induction Furnace Stores Flow

Every kilogram of pig iron, steel scrap, or ferro alloy leaving stores must be authorized by an approved Material Issue Slip tagged with the target Heat Reference Number.

1 Issue Slip Creation

Draft Issue Slip (Stores Dept)

Stores creates an Issue Slip, selects destination department (Foundry), and enters target Heat Number (e.g. H-20261015-0001).

👤 usr_stores_01 Status: DRAFT
2 Material Weighment & Allocation

Line Item Allocation

Stores records exact net weights of Pig Iron, CRCA Scrap, FeSi 75%, FeMn, and Carbon Raiser dispatched to the furnace floor.

👤 usr_stores_01 Status: SUBMITTED
3 Checker Approval & Ledger Posting

Management Approval & Outward Stock Posting

Management approves the issue slip. The stock ledger instantly records MOVEMENT_TYPE = OUTWARD_ISSUE, reducing available inventory balance.

👑 usr_mgmt_01 ⚡ Ledger Balance Deducted
4 Foundry Availability

Live Link to Heat Log Modal

The approved issue slip and its constituent lines are now immediately selectable in the Furnace Heat Log raw material dropdown!

🔥 Foundry Team Ready for Melting
Workflow 3: Induction Furnace Heat Logging & Liquid Heel Mass-Balance Foundry Flow

Tracks induction melting cycles, eliminates "zero melt loss" anomalies, enforces liquid heel carryover, and maps issued raw materials to good castings produced.

1 Opening Heel Verification

Liquid Heel Carryover

Operator clicks "⚡ Propose from Prior Heat" or checks "Cold Start" (0 kg). Measurement method recorded: Dip-rod, Crane-scale difference, or visual estimate.

🔥 usr_foundry_01 Opening Mass Baseline
2 Crucible Charge Selection

Raw Material Selection via Dropdown

Operator selects raw materials from the new dropdown: 📋 Materials Issued to Foundry (auto-fills description, qty, and UOM) or standard master catalog items.

🔥 usr_foundry_01 Gross Charge (kg)
3 Tapped Weight & Closing Heel

Melting Mass-Balance Calculation

Operator inputs Crane-scale ladle tapped metal weight and closing heel left in furnace. Live simulator calculates True Melt Loss and Recovery %.

🔥 usr_foundry_01 Anomaly Gate Check
4 Castings Produced & Allocation

Casting Yield & Heat Commitment

Operator logs parts produced (e.g. SG 400/12 Hubs, GGG 40 Brackets) categorized as GOOD, REJECT, or RUNNERS. System calculates net casting yield %.

🔥 usr_foundry_01 ⚡ Heat Log Committed
Workflow 4: Authoritative Costing Engine & By-Product Recovery Management Flow

Authoritative costing engine computes moving weighted-average costs, credits internal returns/runner-risers at Net Realizable Value (AS 2), and reports finished casting costs.

1 Rate Hierarchy Resolution

Moving Weighted-Average Rates

For every charge item, rate is resolved from: (1) Approved Opening Valuation, (2) Cumulative GRN Receipts, or (3) Validated Material Alias.

👑 usr_mgmt_01 Rate Engine
2 By-Product Scrap Credit (AS 2)

Foundry Returns (Runner/Riser Credit)

Internal runner-riser scrap (FDY-RM-003) recovered from fettling is credited back to heat cost at the approved net recovery rate (e.g. ₹38.00/kg).

👑 Finance Lead - By-Product Credit
3 Exception Handling Queue

Validation & Anomaly Quarantine

Any heats with unmapped charge lines, yield breaches (>8% melt loss), or missing tapped weights are flagged in the Exceptions Queue for resolution.

👑 usr_mgmt_01 Exception Queue
4 Cost Lock & Journal Entries

Unit Cost Per Casting Generated

Authoritative Costing Run generates final cost per tapped kilogram and net cost per finished good casting, permanently locking the costing period.

👑 usr_mgmt_01 ⚡ Costing Run Committed
