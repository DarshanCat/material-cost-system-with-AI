2. How All Has To Be Done (Detailed Operational Guide) SOP Instructions
Step 1 • Master Data
Item Master & Nomenclature
All items follow strict nomenclature: DEP-CAT-XXX (e.g. FDY-RM-001). If an item is missing, Stores submits an Item Code Request. Maker-checker prevents self-approval. Non-standard codes are grandfathered and never silently overwritten.
Rule: Regex ^[A-Z]{3}-[A-Z]{2}-[0-9]{3}$ • Inactive toggle, never delete
Step 2 • Procurement Inward
Goods Receipt Note (GRN)
Stores creates a GRN recording vendor, invoice number, item, and mandatory positive unit_rate_ex_gst. System checks for duplicate invoice numbers. Upon approval by Stores Manager, appends a positive stock movement (+Q) to the ledger.
Authority: post_grn_to_ledger • Taxes recorded separately, not in costing
Step 3 • Physical Issue
Stores Issue Slip Approval
Stores issues raw material upon receiving an authorized slip. System locks stock row with FOR UPDATE, checks available balance, and appends MovementType.ISSUE (-Q). This is the SOLE authoritative point of inventory reduction.
Authority: issue_service.py:198-202 • Negative stock strictly blocked
Step 4 • Foundry Melt Shop
Heat Log & Crucible Charge
Melt shop operators record heat number, furnace, shift, tapped weight, and raw material additions. System translates shop floor nicknames via Material Aliases. Zero stock ledger rows are written, ensuring no duplicate deduction occurs.
Authority: heat_service.py • Captures chemistry, tapped weight & parts
Step 5 • Audit Verification
Three-Pass Reconciliation
Reconciliation compares Stores issue slips against Heat crucible charges. Pass 1 matches exact heat numbers; Pass 2 matches daily batch totals; Pass 3 discovers fuzzy candidates. Flags any weight difference exceeding the 2% tolerance limit.
Tolerance: max(0.5 kg, 0.02 × IssuedQty) • Decimal ratio check ≥9.5
Step 6 • Daily Valuation
MWAR Costing Run Execution
At 13:15 daily, the Costing Engine sorts same-day transactions chronologically (receipts before issues), updates the moving weighted-average rate, computes liquid metal rate per kg, and allocates exact material costs to finished good castings.
Formula: R_new = (Q_prev*R_prev + Q_rcpt*R_rcpt) / (Q_prev + Q_rcpt)
3. Comprehensive Formula Register 10 Verified Formulas
Formula 1 • Stock Balance
Closing Inventory Quantity
Closing Qty = Opening Qty + Σ SignedLedgerQuantity
Calculated from immutable ledger movements (+Receipts, -Issues, -Scrap Outward, +Recovery, ±Adjustments). Silent overwrites and negative balances are forbidden.
Formula 2 • Valuation
Moving Weighted-Average Rate (MWAR)
R_new = [(Q_prev × R_prev) + (Q_rcpt × R_rcpt)] ÷ [Q_prev + Q_rcpt]
Recalculated upon every approved receipt. Rounded to 4 decimal places (0.0001). If previous stock ≤ 0, rate resets to current receipt rate.
Formula 3 • Issue Valuation
Outward Stock Issue Value
Issue Value = round(Issued Qty × R_prev, 2)
Consumes stock at current applicable MWAR rate. Outward issues reduce physical quantity and inventory valuation, leaving the average rate unchanged.
Formula 4 • Heat Aggregation
Charge Aggregation & Variance
Variance (kg) = IssuedQty(heat, item) - ChargeQty(heat, item)
Repeated additions of the same material within a single heat are aggregated before comparison. Excludes non-charge supplies and floor consumables.
Formula 5 • Reconciliation
Reconciliation Tolerance & Anomaly Ratio
Tolerance Limit = max(0.5000 kg, 0.02 × IssuedQty)
Ratio = ChargeQty ÷ IssuedQty
Classified as OK if absolute variance ≤ tolerance. Flagged as DECIMAL_SUSPECT if Ratio ≥ 9.5 or ≤ 0.105 (indicates 10x typo).
Formula 6 • Efficiency
Melt Loss (kg & %)
Melt Loss (kg) = Charge Weight - Metal Tapped Weight
Melt Loss (%) = [Melt Loss (kg) ÷ Charge Weight] × 100
Target range: 3.0% to 6.5%. Loss > 6.5% generates warning flag; loss > 8.0% triggers an immediate QA and scale calibration hold.
Formula 7 • Casting Yield
Good Casting Yield & Liquid Metal Rate
Good Yield (%) = [Σ (Good Parts × Unit Cast Wt) ÷ Tapped Wt] × 100
Liquid Metal Rate/kg = Total Heat Material Cost ÷ Tapped Wt
Dividing total heat cost by tapped weight absorbs melting loss into the liquid metal cost, giving the true cost per kilogram of molten metal.
Formula 8 • Cost Allocation
Finished Part Unit Cost
Unit Part Cost = round(Unit Cast Weight × Liquid Metal Rate, 2)
Allocates liquid metal cost to good parts. Rejection and scrap costs remain accounted for in the net yield rate.
Formula 9 • Baseline Valuation
Opening Stock Valuation
Opening Value = Certified Physical Qty × Approved Opening Unit Cost
Must be supported by certified physical stock count as of 12 October 2026. Cannot be guessed or back-calculated from ledger summaries.
Formula 10 • Item Code Registry
Serial Number Sequence Generator
Next Serial = Highest Ever Issued Serial(dept, category) + 1
Serials are never reused and sequence gaps are never filled. Locked with FOR UPDATE during assignment to prevent concurrent collisions.

7. Exception Handling & Approval Gates Blocker Controls
Exception Condition Required Operational Action System Gate Status
Uncertified Opening Stock Complete formal physical stocktake as of 12 October 2026 cutover with signed management sign-off. BLOCKED Opening balances cannot be posted from summary snapshots.
Ledger-Only Item Code Submit Item Code Generator request with verified description, category, and UOM for Plant Head approval. BLOCKED Bulk insertion of unapproved codes is prohibited.
Missing Issue Slip Reference Retrieve physical paper issue slip from foundry stores archives and enter voucher number. HELD Unlinked issues held from reconciliation match.
UOM Conflict on Item Stores & Engineering confirm base UOM and approved conversion ratio with documented evidence. HELD No automatic or unapproved UOM conversion allowed.
Negative Stock Balance Investigate missing receipt or physical counting error; resolve through authorized GRN or audit entry. BLOCKED Negative stock issues blocked by row lock constraint.
Decimal Scale Suspect (Ratio ≥ 9.5) Audit slip for 10x order-of-magnitude slip typographical errors (e.g. 50 kg entered as 500 kg). AUDIT HOLD Highlighted in reconciliation exception report.
