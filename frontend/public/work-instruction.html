2. The Daily 1 PM – 1 PM Accounting Cycle (SOP Timeline)
13:00 (Day T)
Cycle Start & Handover
Previous 24-hr accounting cycle is archived. New cycle begins tracking all incoming raw materials, scrap, and foundry issues.
13:00 – 12:30 (Day T+1)
Continuous Real-Time Data Capture
Stores records GRNs immediately upon truck unload. Melt Shop operators enter Heat Logs (charging weights, ferroalloys, power KWH, tapped metal) immediately after each tap.
12:30 – 13:00
Pre-Closing Sanity Check
Stores Supervisor audits pending delivery challans. Melting Lead verifies all heats tapped before 12:30 have complete charge slips entered.
13:00 (Day T+1)
HARD CUT-OFF (Transaction Freeze)
Strict system cut-off. Any receipt or issue logged after 13:00:00 automatically rolls into the subsequent day's cycle.
13:15
Automated Cost & Valuation Run
Engine calculates moving weighted-average costs per unit for all items, generates the daily stock journal, and computes net liquid metal cost per kg.
13:20 – 14:00
Anomaly Resolution & Sign-Off Window
Cost Accountant & Plant Head review exceptions (negative stock, abnormal melt loss > 8%, unmapped item codes). Approved ledger snapshot is committed to Finance.
3. Calculation Flow & Valuation Engine (With Worked 500 Kg Heat Example)
Stage 1: Inventory Moving Average
R_new = (Q_curr × R_prev + Q_grn × R_landed) / (Q_curr + Q_grn)
Recalculated upon every accepted GRN. Dispatches are issued at current R_new.
Stage 2: Melt Loss & Yield
Melt Loss % = [(Q_gross - Q_tapped) / Q_gross] × 100
Standard Tolerance: 3.0% – 6.5%. >6.5% triggers warning; >8.0% critical audit hold.
Stage 3: Net Liquid Metal Rate
Rate/Kg = (Cost_RM + Cost_Power + Cost_Consumables) / Q_tapped
Unrecovered metallic loss is absorbed into yield, determining true cost per kg.

5. Standard Operating Procedures (By Role)
Stores Department
Inward & Issue Execution
  • Weigh delivery trucks upon arrival (Gross & Tare).
  • Log GRN in MMS with Supplier Challan No, exact item code (e.g. FDY-RM-001), and landed rate.
  • Ensure quarantine until QA testing releases material.
  • Never dispatch material without approved MMS requisition.
  • Complete all pending day entries prior to 12:30.
Foundry Melt Shop
Heat Log & Charge Logging
  • Record furnace number, target grade (e.g. EN-GJS-500-7), and operator name.
  • Weigh and record exact metallic charge: Pig Iron, CRCA Scrap, Returns.
  • Log ferroalloy additions (FeSi, FeSiMg nodulizer, inoculant, recarburizer).
  • Record starting and finishing KWH meter readings and tap temp.
  • Weigh and log liquid metal tapped for melt loss calculation.
Finance & Cost Accounting
Valuation & Exceptions
  • Enforce 13:00 transaction freeze across plants.
  • Execute material_ledger_engine.py and Cost Run Phase 6 at 13:15.
  • Review Anomaly Register: Negative stock balances, abnormal melt losses (> 6.5%).
  • Sign off daily moving weighted-average valuation.
  • Export official valuation snapshot to ERP / Accounts.
6. Anomaly Resolution & Tolerance Protocol
Anomaly Type Threshold / Condition Operational Cause Resolution Protocol
Negative Stock Calculated balance < 0 Physical issue logged before GRN entry Stores has 30 mins to enter missing GRN or post physical count credit. Escalates to Plant Head at 13:45.
High Melt Loss Melt loss > 6.5% (Warning) > 8% (Critical) Scrap rust, scale error, high slagging Melt supervisor inspects weigh scale calibration and scrap oxidation. Critical heats held for QA review.
Unmapped Alias Vendor challan text unmatched Supplier changed item description Held in quarantine alias queue. Master Data Admin maps alias to standard nomenclature code.
High Power Consumption > 620 KWH / MT liquid metal Refractory lining erosion or delays Furnace maintenance alerted for lining patch inspection.
